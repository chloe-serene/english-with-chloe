# English Teaching Business (Chloe) — working folder

Chloe's **second** business, separate from Serene Vietnam (the travel site). Online conversational English for Vietnamese adults; 1-1 (60 min) + small group of 4–5 (90 min); two levels (Newbie + Intermediate); no exam prep.

## What's here
- **research/COMPETITORS.md** — full competitor research on the 13 competitors from the screenshots (rates, programs, target, platforms, content, USP, quality verdict, T&C, sources).
- **research/STRATEGY.md** — pricing strategy, marketing/content engine, T&C draft, the 2 draft curricula (Newbie + Intermediate), best group-class timing, and the decisions to lock before the website is built.
