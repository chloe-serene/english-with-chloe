## 3. Organic strategy (solo-teacher realistic)

### Phase 0 - Launch prerequisites (blocking)
- **Custom domain** (e.g. dailychatwithchloe.com or .vn). A workers.dev subdomain of a travel brand undermines both trust and rankings. Update canonicals/hreflang, then remove noindex.
- Add **sitemap.xml**, register **Google Search Console** + Bing Webmaster, submit sitemap.
- og-image + real socials in JSON-LD (sameAs) once handles exist.

### Phase 1 (months 1-2): 4 pillar pages
1. "Lộ trình học tiếng Anh giao tiếp cho người đi làm" (Cluster A/B bridge)
2. "Lộ trình học tiếng Anh cho người mất gốc" (Cluster B; the proven-format page every big brand has)
3. "50 câu hỏi phỏng vấn tiếng Anh thường gặp và cách trả lời" (Cluster C traffic magnet)
4. **"Tiếng Anh phỏng vấn tiếp viên hàng không: quy trình từng hãng + kinh nghiệm đậu ngay lần đầu"** - crown jewel: first-hand story (300→37), airline-by-airline detail, nobody else can write this. Target #1 within 3-6 months.
Format for all: answer-first opening (2-3 sentences that directly answer the query - this is what AI search cites), FAQ schema per post, internal links to homepage sections, one CTA to free trial.

### Phase 2 (ongoing): reel ↔ post flywheel
Every TikTok/reel topic becomes a 400-700 word post (embed the video); every post idea becomes a reel. 2 posts/month is sustainable solo. Long-tails from Cluster D. TikTok drives **brand searches** ("Daily Chat with Chloe") which the site must own - easy win, and brand-search volume is itself a ranking signal.

### Links & E-E-A-T (lightweight)
- Author box on every post: Chloe's photo, Switzerland background, student results (E-E-A-T "Experience").
- Links: her TikTok/FB profiles, student-story shares, 2-3 guest posts on VN career/HR blogs, answering questions in FB job-hunt groups with post links (where allowed).
- Skip: paid links, directories spam, GBP (online-only service, marginal).

### Measurement & expectations
- Monthly GSC review: queries, positions, CTR.
- Realistic: head terms (giao tiếp online, mất gốc) are owned by Langmaster/ILA/Prep - win long-tails first, head terms are a 12-month+ game. Cabin-crew cluster: top 3 in 3-6 months is realistic. SEO here is the slow flywheel BEHIND TikTok, not the primary channel; its first job is converting brand searches and interview long-tails.
