## 0. Your plan, in one line
Online **conversational English for Vietnamese adults (23–35, avg+ income)**, **1-1 (60 min)** and **small group of 4–5 (90 min)**, **two levels (Newbie + Intermediate)**, no exam prep. This is a proven, crowded model — you win on **personality + small classes + real structure**, not price.

## 1. Pricing strategy

### Where your prices sit (from the market map in COMPETITORS.md)
| Your product | Your price | Per lesson | Per hour | Market read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1-1** (60 min) | 270–300k/lesson, pay per 10 | 270–300k | 270–300k | **Top of the "professional VN teacher" band** (giáo viên rate 270–300k). Above budget tutors (65–170k), below natives (500k+). Legit — but you must justify it. |
| **Group 4–5** (90 min) | 5,500k / 30 lessons | ~183k | ~122k | **Above center group per-lesson**, but for a *much smaller* class. Defensible only if you sell "talk time per student." |

### Recommendations
1. **Keep 1-1 at 300k, but make it the premium/credibility tier, not the volume tier.** At 300k×60min it must feel bespoke: personalized roadmap, recording after each lesson, homework feedback. Consider a **small intro discount for the first block** (e.g. first 10 lessons at 250k) to lower the trial barrier, then 300k.
2. **The group class is your profit engine — protect and promote it.** Math: a full group of 5 = 27,500k ÷ 30 lessons ≈ **917k per 90-min lesson taught**, vs 300k for a 1-1 hour. Filling groups is 3× more valuable than 1-1 per hour of your time. So: **1-1 is the funnel & the flex; groups are the business.**
3. **Sell the group on the ≤5 promise.** Your headline vs centers: *"Max 5 students — so you actually speak every lesson. Centers pack 6–10."* That single line justifies the higher per-lesson price. (Nana caps at 4; The Language Planet 4–8; Simple 6–10 — you sit in the premium-small band.)
4. **Payment cadence — good, with one tweak.**
   - 1-1 **per 10 lessons** ✓ (keeps commitment low, ~2.7–3M chunks). Keep.
   - Group **5,500k / 30 lessons upfront** = a 5.5M barrier. **Offer two options:** (a) pay in full = keep 5,500k (or a tiny "pay-in-full" perk), (b) **2 installments** (e.g. 2 × 2,900k) to reduce friction — you still secure the cohort. Full-upfront is better for cashflow & retention; installments widen the funnel. Test both.
5. **Founding-cohort pricing (to fill your FIRST group fast):** first group ever = **"Founding Class" at ~30% off (e.g. 3,900k)** in exchange for **a testimonial + permission to use their progress in content.** You're buying your first proof. Do this ONCE, be transparent it's a launch price.
6. **Discount guardrails:** never discount the 1-1 headline publicly (protects the premium signal). Do time-boxed *cohort* promos and *referral* credits instead of standing discounts (EEC's permanent −54% teaches shoppers to wait — don't train that).
7. **A light guarantee (borrowed from Simple English, de-risked for a solo teacher):** *"If after your first paid block you don't feel more confident, we'll refund the unused lessons, no drama."* Cheap to offer (few claim it), and it removes the #1 objection for a new, unreviewed teacher.

> **To finalize pricing:** mystery-shop 2–3 solo competitors (Nana, Trang Nim, The Language Planet) for their actual rates — see the gap note in COMPETITORS.md — then we lock numbers.

## 2. Marketing strategy (0 → first students)

### The engine: short-form video. Full stop.
Every competitor with real reach got it from Reels/TikTok — often with **more views than followers** (Funny IELTS: 94K views from 16 posts). This is the cheapest, fastest channel and it rewards *consistency over polish*. Your daily job for 90 days:

**Post one short reel every day**, rotating these four proven formats (all lifted from the research):
1. **Situational phrases** (Nana's format): "AT THE COFFEE SHOP — 5 phrases you actually need." One scene, 4–6 phrases, VN subtitle. *Most copyable, most useful.*
2. **One useful word, VN→EN** (Trang Nim's format): "*Reschedule* = đổi lịch. Use it: '…'." 15 seconds. *Lowest effort, highest volume — your daily default.*
3. **Challenge / myth-bust** (Funny IELTS's format): "Người Việt hay nói sai câu này", "Do natives really talk this fast, or are you listening wrong?" *Highest reach ceiling — post 1–2/week.*
4. **Aspirational identity** (Aurora's format): "Học tiếng Anh không phải để giỏi tiếng Anh — mà để…" *Builds the WHY and attracts your 23–35 professional. 1/week.*

**Rules:** you on camera (the teacher IS the brand here — faceless pages don't grow), same hook style, VN captions, a clear "comment/DM 'HỌC' để nhận lộ trình" CTA on value posts only. Batch-film weekly, post daily.

### The funnel (what turns a viewer into a paying student)
`Reel → profile/bio → free level test + 1 free trial lesson → Zalo consult (personalized roadmap) → 1-1 block OR group cohort → referral`
- **Free trial lesson + free level test** are table-stakes in this market (EEC, Simple, Ms Thanh all offer them). Make yours the moment they fall for your teaching.
- **Zalo is where the sale happens** in Vietnam — put it everywhere; respond fast.
- **A simple website** (Phase 2) sets you apart from every solo teacher (none have one) and gives Zalo/DM a credible link to send.

### Positioning wedge (say this everywhere)
> *"A real teacher who knows your name, classes capped at 5, a clear plan from lesson 1 — not a center, not a random tutor."*
This threads the needle: solo warmth + institutional structure. It's the white space nobody in your lane fills cleanly.

### First-30-days plan (mirrors what worked for the travel business)
- Week 1: set up FB + TikTok, film 7 reels, open Zalo, define the free-trial flow, build a warm list (friends, colleagues, ex-classmates who want to practice).
- Daily: 1 reel + reply to every comment/DM within the hour.
- Fill the **Founding Group** first (offer above) → those students' testimonials + clips become week-3 content.

**Rescheduling & cancellation (1-1)**
- **Free reschedule** with **≥24 hours** notice.
- **Less than 24 hours or a no-show:** the lesson is deducted in full (100%).

**Refunds**
- **Confidence guarantee (optional, recommended):** if after your **first paid block** you're not satisfied, unused lessons are refunded.

**Conduct & recordings**
- Be on time and prepared; lessons may be recorded for your own review. We never share your recordings without consent. Respectful conduct is expected in group classes.

## 4. Full fixed programs (draft curricula)

Two levels, each a **30-lesson course** (matches your group course length). In **1-1** the same syllabus is delivered in 60-min lessons sold per 10 (so a level ≈ 3 blocks). In **group** it's 30 × 90-min lessons. Each level splits into **3 modules of 10 lessons.**

**Every lesson follows the same shape** (so it's teachable and consistent):
`Warm-up chat (5–10m) → Target language: phrases/pronunciation (15m) → Guided practice (15m) → Free speaking / role-play (longer in group, 90m) → Feedback + homework (5m)`

### Course A — **NEWBIE ("Survive & Connect", A1 → A2)**
Outcome: handle everyday situations and basic self-expression without freezing.
- **Module 1 — Foundations & Me (L1–10):** English sounds & rhythm (pronunciation reset); greetings & introductions; talking about yourself/family/job; numbers, time, dates, money; daily routines; asking & giving personal info; "I don't understand / can you repeat?"; likes & dislikes; describing where you live; Module review + role-play (meeting someone new).
- **Module 2 — Getting Around Daily Life (L11–20):** directions & transport; ordering food & drinks; shopping & prices; making appointments & plans; phone & messaging basics; at the doctor/pharmacy; describing people & things; weather & small talk; polite requests; Module review + role-play (a day out).
- **Module 3 — Simple Stories & Connection (L21–30):** past simple — "what I did on the weekend"; future plans ("going to"); giving simple opinions; comparing things; expressing feelings; agreeing/disagreeing simply; telling a short story; handling misunderstandings; a mini "meet a foreigner" simulation; **final free-talk assessment + roadmap to Intermediate.**

### Course B — **INTERMEDIATE ("Speak with Confidence", A2 → B1+)**
Outcome: hold real conversations at work and socially, with fluency and natural phrasing.
- **Module 1 — Fluency & Opinions (L1–10):** connected speech & fillers (sound natural, buy thinking time); giving & justifying opinions; agreeing/disagreeing politely; storytelling with mixed tenses; describing experiences; small talk → deeper conversation; talking about news/trends; expressing uncertainty; Module review + discussion.
- **Module 2 — English for Work (L11–20):** introducing yourself professionally; updates & status in meetings; asking questions & clarifying; presenting an idea; disagreeing/pushing back politely; handling calls & online meetings; talking about your industry; networking small talk; writing-you-say (spoken email/messages); Module review + meeting simulation.
- **Module 3 — Natural & Nuanced (L21–30):** idioms & natural phrasing; humor & culture; negotiating & complaining politely; hypotheticals ("if I were you…"); describing data/trends; debate & persuasion; interview practice; giving a short talk; reacting spontaneously (improv); **final confidence assessment + next-steps plan.**

> **1-1 flex:** in private lessons, swap any module's theme for the student's real need (their job, an upcoming trip, an interview) — that personalization is exactly what justifies the 300k price.

## 5. Best time to run group classes (recruiting working adults)

**Evidence (from Flying English's published schedule — the one competitor whose class times are visible):** working-adult classes cluster at **weekday evenings** — 18:45–20:15 and 20:30–22:00 (Tue/Thu, Wed/Sat pairs) — plus weekends.

**Recommended launch slots (offer 2, let demand decide):**
- **Slot 1 — Tue & Thu, 20:00–21:30** (after dinner; the reliable workhorse slot for 9-to-6 office workers).
- **Slot 2 — Sat & Sun, 9:30–11:00** (weekend-morning learners who won't commit on weeknights).
- Backup / overflow: **Mon & Wed 20:00–21:30**, or **Sat 19:30–21:00**.

**Why 90-min twice-weekly works:** two touches/week keeps momentum without weeknight burnout; 90 min gives each of ≤5 students real speaking time.

**Recruiting cadence:** run **fixed monthly intakes** (e.g. "next Newbie group starts 1st of the month") rather than rolling starts — a deadline drives sign-ups, and cohorts that start together bond and retain better. **Poll your audience** ("weeknight or weekend?") in a reel/story before finalizing — it doubles as content and market research.

## 6. Extra advice

- **Brand/name:** decide before the website. Options in this market: *your own name* (like Nana, Ms Huyền — maximizes the personal-brand advantage) or a *studio name* (like The Language Planet — feels more established, easier to scale/hire later). Given the market rewards the person, **lean toward a name that features you** (e.g. "[YourName] English" or "English with [YourName]") with a warm tagline.
- **On camera:** the uncomfortable truth — the winners are all on camera. If you're willing, it's your single biggest growth lever. If you're not (yet), start with voice + text-on-screen reels and ease in.
- **Retention & referral loop:** at the end of every block, (1) ask for a short testimonial, (2) offer a **referral credit** ("bring a friend to your group, get 1 lesson free"), (3) sell the next level. Referrals are the cheapest students you'll ever get.
- **Free community hook (cheap acquisition):** a free **Zalo/FB speaking group** or a monthly free "practice call" (à la Khu Phố's community model) keeps warm leads close until they buy.
- **Teaching ops:** pick Zoom vs Google Meet, a simple booking method (even a Google Sheet + Zalo to start), and a payment method students trust (bank transfer / MoMo / ZaloPay).
