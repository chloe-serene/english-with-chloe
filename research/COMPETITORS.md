## 1. The Market Map (the one-screen takeaway)

The Vietnamese "tiếng Anh giao tiếp" (conversational English) market splits into **three tiers**, and you're choosing which one to play in:

| Tier | Who | Price/lesson (1-1) | How they win |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget tutors** | Students, junior tutors, low-cost apps (Kyna) | 🟢 65k–170k | Cheap, "learn 1-1 without spending a fortune" |
| **Professional solo teachers/coaches** ← *your tier* | Experienced teachers with a personal brand (Nana, Ms Huyền, Trang Nim, Ms Thanh) | 🟢 ~270k–300k (giáo viên rate) | Personal trust, results, personality, content |
| **Centers / systems** | EEC, Simple English, Flying English, Khu Phố, The Language Planet | 🟢 bundled ~90k–170k/lesson or ~1.3M/month | Brand, reviews (1,000+), guarantees, scale |
| **Native-speaker premium** | Native 1-1 | 🟢 500k+/hour | "Real" native exposure |

Sources: giasuttv.net (HCMC tutor rates: giáo viên 270–300k/buổi), kyna-english.vn (natives 500k+/hr), edu2review (center bundles), FB tutor posts (65k–600k/mo). URLs in §5.

**What this means for you (short version):**
1. Your **1-1 at 270–300k/lesson is a legitimate "professional teacher" price — but it is 2–4× the budget tutors and above most center per-lesson math.** You are NOT competing on price; you must sell *personality + results + personalization*. That's exactly what the successful solo brands (Nana, Trang Nim) do.
2. Your **group at ~183k/lesson (5,500k ÷ 30) for a 4–5 person class** is priced *above* center group courses per-lesson, but your group is *smaller* (more speaking time each). That's defensible — but it must be the headline you sell ("max 5 students = 3× more talk time than a center's 6–10").
3. **Everybody wins on the same channel: short-form video (Reels/TikTok).** Every single competitor with real reach got it from situational-phrase reels or challenge videos (60K–2.5M views). This is the growth engine — see STRATEGY.md.
4. **White space you can own:** the solo coaches are strong on *content* but thin on *structure/trust* (no clear curriculum, no visible policy, few reviews). The centers are strong on *trust* but impersonal (groups of 6–10, generic). **Your wedge = a solo teacher's warmth + a center's structure**: a clearly-published curriculum, a small-group promise (≤5), a written policy, and a free trial. Nobody in your exact lane is doing all four cleanly.

## 3. Direct competitors — solo teachers & coaches (your real peers)

### 1. The Language Planet — *your closest structural twin* 🥇
- **Programs (🟡):** Two named courses — **"Lớp Tiếng Anh Giao tiếp"** (conversational) and **"Lớp Tiếng Anh Công việc"** (business) — each at **Beginner & Intermediate** (exactly your 2-level idea). Online groups of **4–8 students**, **3 months / 24 sessions**. Also 1:1.
- **Target:** working adults ("người đi làm"), people who need to speak with foreign colleagues/clients or are going to study/live abroad.
- **Platforms:** Instagram (@thelanguageplanet, 55 posts, 1.4K followers, 57 following), Threads. Small but tightly branded.
- **Content:** class-info carousels, lesson snippets ("How to push back on your boss professionally", warm-up discussion cards), teacher intros, feedback highlights ("we care", "classes"). Professional, aesthetic, English-first captions. Engagement modest (small following).
- **USP (🟡):** teachers with **IELTS 7.0–7.5 + TEFL/TESOL**, 3–8 yrs experience; "smart vocabulary + dictionary building"; **max speaking practice + personalized feedback**; a bigger world starts with better English.
- **T&C:** 🔴 not public (DM).
- **Quality:** high production/brand quality, clearly the same product you're planning, just group-led. **Verdict: DIRECT — study this one hardest.**
- **Takeaway for you:** their structure (2 courses × 2 levels × 24 sessions) is a proven template. But their following is small — you can out-market them on video and out-personalize them with 1-1 + a ≤5 group. Copy their *clarity*, beat their *reach*.

### 2. Nana EnglishTutor — *your model twin* 🥈
- **Programs (🟡):** IPA pronunciation course + **conversational English A1–B2+**; **1:1 (personalized by goal)** and **small group max 4** ("tối đa 4 học viên, tương tác hiệu quả"), personalized roadmap. For students, uni students, and working people.
- **Platforms:** Facebook (47K followers/likes, 93 posts). Very active on Reels.
- **Content (🟡 — very effective):** situational-phrase reels — **"AT THE HOTEL", "CLOTHES SHOPPING", "AT THE CAFE / RESTAURANT"** — teaching real phrases for a scene. Consistently **60K–81K views**. This is the single most copyable content format for you.
- **USP:** IPA/pronunciation focus + situational practicality + personalized small groups. Warm, approachable teacher persona ("Be confident, Be you!").
- **T&C:** 🔴 DM.
- **Quality:** excellent. Same exact model as yours (1:1 + ≤4 group, conversational, adults). **Verdict: DIRECT — your #1 content benchmark.**
- **Takeaway:** her "situational phrases in one scene" reel format is your content playbook. Her group cap (4) validates your ≤5 promise.

## 6. Cross-cutting findings (what the whole market tells you)

1. **Content format is solved for you.** The winning formats, ranked by leverage for a new page:
   (a) **situational-phrase reels** (Nana: "At the hotel / Clothes shopping") — most copyable;
   (b) **one-useful-word VN→EN reels** (Trang Nim: "reschedule = đổi lịch") — lowest effort, high volume;
   (c) **challenge reels** (Funny IELTS: "can a teacher do 9.0 on X") — highest ceiling for reach;
   (d) **aspirational-identity reels** (Aurora: "learn English to be independent") — builds the *why*.
2. **The teacher IS the brand.** Every solo winner is on camera with a clear persona and a credibility story (years abroad, lecturer, results). Faceless pages don't grow here.
3. **Free trial lesson + free level test = table stakes.** EEC, Simple, Ms Thanh all lead with it. You should too.
4. **Trust is the institutions' moat, personality is the solo teachers'.** You can borrow trust cheaply: a published curriculum, a written policy, a light guarantee, and *visible* early reviews.
5. **Nobody in your exact lane cleanly combines** small-group (≤5) + published structure + written policy + strong video + free trial. **That combination is your opening.**
6. **Platform reality:** Facebook + TikTok/Reels drive discovery; **Zalo** is where the sale/consult happens; a **website** adds legitimacy (only EEC/Simple/Khu Phố have one — a good site would set you apart from the solo teachers).
