Your path

Choose the course that fits you best

A1

Level 1 (A1): Foundations

For learners starting from zero: clean pronunciation from day one; greetings and introducing yourself; short everyday conversations. Goal: speak simple sentences with confidence, without fearing mistakes.

“Hi! I’m Linh. Nice to meet you!”A sentence you'll say with confidence
A2

Level 2 (A2): Building

At this level you practise handling very ordinary things: shopping, booking, asking directions, ordering food, and then move on to chatting about work and hobbies. By the end, the goal is holding a natural conversation for 5–10 minutes.

“I love my job, but weekends are for coffee with friends.”A sentence you'll say with confidence
B1

Level 3 (A2+ to B1): Confident

Giving & defending opinions; telling stories; conversations at work; more natural phrasing. Goal: communicate comfortably in real life and at work.

“In my opinion, we should try a different approach.”A sentence you'll say with confidence

Not sure which level you're at? Take the free level test, and Chloe will place you in the right class.
