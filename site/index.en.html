Levels

Level 1 (A1) — Foundations

Build accurate pronunciation from the start; greetings, introductions, and short daily conversations.

Goal: start conversations confidently and use simple sentences without fear of mistakes.

Level 2 (A2) — Building

Daily situations (shopping, appointments, directions, ordering food); small talk; your work and hobbies.

Goal: hold a natural 5–10 minute conversation.

Level 3 (A2+–B1) — Confident

Give and defend opinions; tell stories; workplace conversations; express yourself more naturally.

Goal: communicate comfortably in daily life and at work.
